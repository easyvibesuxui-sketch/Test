    Hadal House · Archive of the deep · Est. 1887

    Will it surface, or will it sleep?

    The deep sea is the largest unread library on Earth. Fishermen, sonar crews and divers have been adding to it for centuries, one half-believed story at a time.

    At Hadal House we keep only two kinds of legends: the ones that are rising, and the ones that should be left asleep.

      The keepers.

      A small, stubborn archive in a decommissioned lighthouse on the North Atlantic, founded in 1887 by a harbour master who kept a second logbook for things he couldn't explain. Today we hold 1,140 legends, 312 sonar recordings and one jar of ink nobody will open. We take sightings from anyone, at any depth.
