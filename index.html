02 — Anatomy

Built to be
opened once.

  1. 01

    Bottom shell

    Glass-fibre reinforced polymer tray with integrated mounting flanges and a sealed cable gland. Bolted straight onto the machine frame.

  2. 02

    Main board

    LTE-M / NB-IoT modem, multi-constellation GNSS receiver and a CAN transceiver on one board. Firmware arrives over the air.

  3. 03

    Power & antenna

    Ceramic GNSS patch and a backup lithium cell. The device keeps reporting when the machine battery is disconnected.

  4. 04

    IP68 seal

    A compression-moulded silicone gasket rated for dust, jet water and the pressure washer at the yard.

  5. 05

    Top shell

    UV-stable housing with the status window. Shock resistant, vibration tested — and invisible once it is on the machine.

03 — The lineup

One box for every
asset on site.

From a 40-tonne excavator on the CAN bus down to a vibratory plate with no power supply at all. Every device reports into the same platform.

CORE

Wired · CAN bus

Developed for vehicles where vehicle data, CAN bus information and usage analysis are crucial. Ideal for large construction machines.

  • 1 positive + 3 negative trigger inputs
  • Analog inputs 0–16 V and 0–30 V
  • Digital output, open drain, 150 mA
  • Installed by screw connection, power or CAN
01

EDGE

Plug & play

Connected to the power source of the machine plug-and-play. Suitable for equipment with its own supply, focused on operating hours and location.

  • No workshop time, no wiring loom
  • Operating hours recorded automatically
  • Live position and movement alarms
  • Moves with the machine, not the fleet
02

LITE

Live location

The solution for machinery where only the real-time GPS location is required. Compact, easy to install, connected directly to the battery.

  • Precise live location data
  • Automatic theft alarm
  • Direct battery connection
  • Smallest wired footprint
03

LINK & LINK mini

Attachments

For attachments and machines with no internal power supply — buckets, separators, vibratory plates. Robust, weatherproof, riveted on in minutes.

  • LTE-M and NB-IoT transmission
  • Replaceable batteries
  • Operating hours tracking & recording
  • Installation by riveting
04

VOLT

Up to 5 years

A GPS tracker with particularly long battery life for trailers and mobile objects without a power supply of their own.

  • Battery life up to 5 years
  • Up to 4 months with no external power
  • Automatic theft alarm
  • Weatherproof, magnetic mounting
05

SOLAR

Self-powered

Solar-powered tracking for containers, construction fences and other non-electrified assets. Fit it once and forget it.

  • Live GPS location, real-time tracking
  • Movement alarm when the container moves
  • Up to 3 months without direct sunlight
  • Simple installation on containers
06

OBD

Fleet vehicles

The OBD-II connection supplies the device with power and enables OBD-II data to be read out. Straight into the port, no tools.

  • ISO 15765 data read-out
  • Legally mandated protocols supported
  • Vehicles built 2008 or later (USA)
  • Transferable between vehicles
07

DOT · passive

Small devices

Passive telematics for small devices and hand tools. Scanned at handover, so even items without electronics stay in the inventory.

  • No battery, no maintenance
  • Scan on check-in and check-out
  • Digital handover report
  • Cheapest way to close the gaps

04 — What comes back

The data your
fleet is already producing.

All machine telematics data is transmitted to SAM & RAM in real time and is available in the app, on the map and in every report.

Live location

Every machine on the map at any time, with the position history behind it. You always know which site an asset is standing on.

Geofencing & alarms

Draw the site boundary once. Notifications trigger the moment a device leaves the defined area or starts moving out of hours.

Operating hours

Movements and operating hours are documented automatically — the basis for invoicing, utilisation and rental settlement.

CAN bus data

With CORE, the machine's own bus is read out: operating hours, speed, fuel consumption and error codes for precise analysis.

Theft protection

GPS tracking, geofencing and real-time notifications detect and report unusual movement immediately — day or night.

Maintenance intervals

Service windows follow the real operating hours instead of the calendar, so inspections land when the machine has earned them.
