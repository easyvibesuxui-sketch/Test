Manifesto

Some technologies pull the future forward. Others keep the lights on when everything else fails. We invest in both.

Not apps at the surface, but the fabric underneath: the protocols, the chemistry, the machines, and the mathematics that everything else will one day depend on. Small teams, unreasonable ambitions, first cheques.

Thesis · 01

An internet nobody owns.

Permissionless networks let anyone build without asking. We back the protocols, infrastructure, and cryptography returning the internet to its users — from decentralized storage and compute to open financial rails.

ProtocolsCryptographyStorageCompute

Thesis · 02

Biology, programmable.

Nature is becoming an engineering discipline. We support founders turning cells, proteins, and chemistry into design spaces — for longer, healthier lives and for industry that works with the planet instead of against it.

Protein designDigital chemistryHealth

Thesis · 03

Engineering the impossible.

Planetary-scale problems need more than software. We fund breakthrough engineering and computation — fusion energy, advanced materials, new kinds of machines — the hard, physical work the next century runs on.

EnergyMaterialsComputationAerospace

Thesis · 04

Knowledge wants out.

The world's data and science are locked in silos and monopolies. We invest in the liberators — teams building open access to information, tools that untangle software lock-in, and machines that reason over what we know.

Open dataApplied AIDeveloper tools
