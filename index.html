            How deep is deep?

            The deepest known point in the ocean, the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, lies about 10,935 m down: Mount Everest would fit inside with two kilometres of water to spare. Roughly three quarters of the sea floor has still not been mapped in high resolution, and most of what we know about the animals here comes from robot submersibles, baited cameras dropped to the floor and tags that ride on whales and sharks.
